# 🤖 AI for Scrum Masters

> **Learn → Experiment → Apply → Reflect → Share**

This repository documents my practical learning journey into **Artificial Intelligence for Scrum and Agile practices**.

The goal is not to replace the Scrum Master's human skills with AI, but to explore how AI can be used responsibly to support:

* 📝 Backlog Refinement
* 🎯 Sprint Planning
* 🤝 Agile Facilitation
* 🧭 Agile Coaching
* 🔍 Impediment & Risk Analysis
* 🔄 Sprint Retrospectives
* 📊 Agile Metrics
* 💬 Stakeholder Communication
* 📚 Documentation
* 🚀 Continuous Improvement

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

I am exploring AI tools that can help Scrum Masters reduce repetitive work, analyse information, prepare for Scrum events, and generate additional perspectives for Agile problem-solving.

The focus of this repository is **practical application**, not just theoretical knowledge.

For each AI tool, I will:

1. Learn the tool and its relevant capabilities
2. Apply it to realistic Scrum/Agile scenarios
3. Experiment with different prompts
4. Evaluate the AI-generated output
5. Apply Scrum Master judgment to the recommendations
6. Document what worked and what didn't
7. Share selected learnings on LinkedIn

# 🧠 Responsible AI Usage

AI should be treated as an **assistant, not the decision-maker**.

Throughout this project, I will focus on:

* Validating AI-generated information
* Protecting confidential and sensitive information
* Avoiding the use of confidential company data in public AI tools
* Considering organizational AI policies
* Validating recommendations against Scrum principles
* Applying human judgment
* Preserving psychological safety
* Avoiding the use of AI to unfairly evaluate individual team members

### Guiding Principle

> **AI can provide suggestions and insights. The Scrum Master remains responsible for understanding the context and deciding how those insights should be applied.**
