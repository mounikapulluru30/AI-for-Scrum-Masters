# 🤖 ChatGPT for Scrum Masters

This section documents my practical learning and experiments with **ChatGPT** to understand how AI can support Scrum Master activities.

## 🎯 Areas I'm Exploring

- Backlog Refinement
- Sprint Planning
- Agile Coaching
- Sprint Retrospectives
- Stakeholder Communication
- Impediment & Risk Analysis
- Continuous Improvement

## 🧪 Learning Approach

For each scenario, I will:

1. Define a realistic Scrum situation
2. Create an AI prompt
3. Analyze the AI response
4. Evaluate the response from a Scrum Master perspective
5. Identify what AI did well
6. Identify what AI missed
7. Define the final Scrum Master approach
8. Document my key learning

## ⚖️ Important Principle

> AI is an assistant, not the decision-maker.

The Scrum Master's understanding of the team, context, people, organizational environment, and Scrum principles remains essential when evaluating AI-generated recommendations.
