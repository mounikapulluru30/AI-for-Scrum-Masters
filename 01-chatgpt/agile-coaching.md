# Agile Coaching Experiment: Product Owner Changes Priorities During the Sprint

## 1. Scenario

A Scrum Team works in two-week Sprints.

The Product Owner frequently changes priorities during the Sprint and asks Developers to stop their current work and start higher-priority items.

The Scrum Master needs to understand the situation, identify possible causes and risks, and determine an appropriate coaching approach.

## 2. Objective

Explore how ChatGPT can assist a Scrum Master in:

- Understanding the possible reasons behind frequent priority changes
- Identifying risks to the Sprint Goal
- Understanding the impact on the Scrum Team
- Preparing effective coaching questions
- Exploring possible facilitation approaches

The objective is not to blindly follow AI recommendations, but to evaluate them using Scrum principles and Scrum Master judgment.

## 3. AI Prompt — Situation Analysis

> Act as an experienced Agile Coach and Scrum Master.
>
> A Scrum Team works in two-week Sprints. The Product Owner frequently changes priorities during the Sprint and asks Developers to stop their current work and start higher-priority items.
>
> Analyze this situation from a Scrum perspective.
>
> Identify:
> 1. Possible underlying reasons for this behavior
> 2. Risks to the Sprint Goal
> 3. Impact on Developers and the Scrum Team
> 4. Questions the Scrum Master should ask before taking action
> 5. What the Scrum Master should avoid doing
>
> Do not immediately assume that the Product Owner is behaving incorrectly. Consider different possible contexts.
